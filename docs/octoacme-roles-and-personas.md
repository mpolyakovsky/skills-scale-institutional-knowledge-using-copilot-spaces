# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide senior-level ownership for a project's business purpose and organizational support.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment and champion the intended business outcomes
- Secure or escalate decisions about funding, priority, and major scope changes
- Remove organizational blockers that are outside the delivery team's authority

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Align with the Product Manager on desired outcomes and priority trade-offs
- Partner with the Project Manager on escalations, sponsorship, and stakeholder updates
- Receive recommendations from the delivery team and make decisions when authority is needed

---

## Engineering Leads / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads guide technical direction and coordinate the engineering approach for a project or workstream.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture and technical design decisions
- Coordinate technical estimates, implementation sequencing, and engineering dependencies
- Identify technical risks and plan mitigations
- Promote maintainability, reliability, and testability

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to assess feasibility and explain technical trade-offs
- Work with the Project Manager to plan dependencies, sequencing, and delivery risks
- Guide Developers during implementation and collaborate with QA/Testing on testability and technical validation

---

## UX / Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads ensure that solutions address user needs and provide a coherent, usable experience.

### Responsibilities
- Lead user research, interaction design, and design validation
- Translate user needs into flows, prototypes, and design guidance
- Identify usability risks and clarify user-facing acceptance criteria

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to define user problems and validate product direction
- Collaborate with Developers and the Engineering Lead on feasible implementation details
- Work with QA/Testing to include usability and experience checks in validation

---

## Security and Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads advise teams on protecting systems and data and meeting applicable security and privacy requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security, privacy, and compliance requirements and risks
- Recommend controls and mitigations, and advise on security readiness
- Coordinate security and privacy reviews or assessments when needed

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to include security and privacy needs in requirements
- Partner with the Engineering Lead and Developers on design choices and mitigations
- Coordinate with the Project Manager to track risks and escalate unresolved issues, and with QA/Testing to plan relevant validation

---

## Release / Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release / Operations Leads coordinate operational readiness and the transition of changes into supported environments.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment readiness, operational monitoring, and rollback planning
- Confirm runbooks, alerts, and operational ownership are prepared as appropriate
- Support incident response and capture operational follow-up actions

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Align release timing and readiness actions with the Project Manager
- Coordinate with Developers, the Engineering Lead, and QA/Testing on deployment and verification plans
- Work with Customer Support / Customer Success Leads on operational handoff and customer-impact information

---

## Customer Support / Customer Success Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Support / Customer Success Leads represent customer-facing needs during delivery and prepare teams to support changes.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer feedback, common questions, and support trends
- Identify customer-facing impacts and support readiness needs
- Prepare support materials and communicate known issues or changes to customer-facing teams

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to inform problem definition and prioritization
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on customer communications and rollout timing
- Work with the Release / Operations Lead on support handoff and incident information

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads help teams define and use evidence to measure project outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define measurement plans and instrumentation needs for success metrics
- Advise on data quality, metric definitions, and analysis
- Report on outcomes and help identify opportunities for iteration

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to connect success metrics to product outcomes
- Coordinate with Developers and the Engineering Lead on instrumentation
- Work with the Project Manager to include measurement milestones in delivery plans and status reporting

---

## Applying Roles to a Project
- Select roles based on project scope, risk, and required expertise; not every project needs a separate person for each role.
- One person may cover multiple roles when appropriate, but make responsibilities and decision ownership explicit.
- Coordinate role assignments with the Project Manager and Product Manager, and identify any expertise gaps early.
